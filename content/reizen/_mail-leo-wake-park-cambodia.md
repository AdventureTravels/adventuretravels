**Onderwerp:** Wakeboarding week packages from the Netherlands — rates and availability

Hi Leo,

I run AdventureTravels, a small Dutch travel company that sells active sports trips, with wakeboarding
as our starting point. We are putting together a one-week wakeboarding trip to Siem Reap for Dutch
travellers and we would like to sell it with your park as the base: guests stay in your lakeside
bungalows, ride on your cable all week, and take one day off for Angkor.

We would run it from November through March, for individual travellers rather than groups, with a
minimum of two people per bungalow. Before we can publish anything, we need your rates and your terms.

**1. One total price per person.** This is the main thing we need. Could you quote us a net package
price per person, based on two people sharing, for:

- 7 nights in a Lakeside Bungalow, and the same for a Lakeside Pool Bungalow;
- a 1 Week Unlimited cable pass;
- breakfast every day.

And, as separate options on top of that, a price with lunch added, and a price with both lunch and
dinner. That way we can decide whether to sell the week half board or full board.

If your rates differ by season, please send them per season. Also let us know how the 15% guest
discount on tickets works in a package like this: is it applied to the week pass, or is it already
part of the net rate you quote us?

**2. What the week pass includes.** Your ticket page lists an instructor, a basic wakeboard, water ski,
kneeboard, life jacket and helmet. Does the same apply to the weekly pass, for the whole week? We want
to state on our website exactly what a guest gets, and we do not want to promise anything you have not
confirmed.

**3. Availability.** How many of the eight bungalows could you hold for us per week between November
and March, and how far in advance? We would like a fixed number of rooms we can sell without asking
you each time, with a release deadline that works for you.

**4. Terms and conditions.** Could you send us, in writing:

- check-in and check-out times;
- your cancellation and payment terms, and whether you require a deposit;
- house rules, and your policy on children;
- any minimum age or swimming requirement for the cable, and whether riders sign a waiver.

We are legally required to publish cancellation terms with every trip we sell in the Netherlands, and
we want ours to match yours rather than guess.

**5. Transfers and Angkor.** Can you arrange airport transfers, and a guided day at Angkor Wat
(guide, tuktuk and entrance pass)? If so, at what price per person? If not, we will book those
ourselves, but we would rather keep it in one hand.

**6. How busy does it get?** What are weekends and Khmer holidays like on the cable? We tell our guests
honestly what to expect, so if there are days with a wait, we would like to say so.

**7. Photos and video.** Your copyright notice says nothing of yours may be republished without written
permission, which we respect. Could you give us written permission to use your photos and video for
this trip, and share the files? All the text on our page is our own.

One small thing: your accommodation page says the park is "just 1 hour from Bangkok". I think that line
was copied from somewhere, since you are in Siem Reap — you may want to correct it.

If it is easier to go through this on a call, I am happy to schedule one.
